PRIORITY 3 — RIGHT HAND TEXTURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

T0 SILENCE: RH empty, LH alone. The world established before story begins.
  Length depends on the piece — could be 2 bars, 4 bars, 6 bars, 12 bars.
  There is no fixed rule. Let the style and reference guide the choice.

T1 SPARSE MOTIF: 3-7 notes/bar. APPROACH (ornament) + INNER (peak) + TAIL (sustain d=8-12).
  Neighbor ornament: X→X-1→X / X→X+1→X / X→X-1→hold
  Motif variation Aa = compressed approach + extended tail. Used as closing signal or exhale.
  Section sequencing: [A A A Aa] / [A A Aa A A Aa] / [A A B Aa] are proven patterns.
  But the piece may use completely different phrase lengths — follow the reference DNA.

T2 LYRICAL SUSTAINED: 2 attacks/bar. s0: melody d=6. s6: pedal d=10.
  Single notes first, then interval pairs (5ths/6ths — avoid 7ths/2nds for long sustains).
  Pedal tone = 5th of most common chord (compatible across the whole cycle).

T3 RAPID ARPEGGIO: all d=1, fill all subdivisions. 3-note rotation: [a,b,c]×5 + [a,b,X,b].
  X at s14 = upper neighbor (prevents mechanical feel). Omit 3rd for open ambiguous sound.
  4-note variant: SUMMIT at s0 and s12 framing. 3→4 note transition signals escalation.
  After 8+ bars of T3, a breath section must follow (T2 or T0).

T4 INTERVAL WEAVE: 
  VERSION A: two simultaneous notes (3rds/4ths/5ths/6ths), d=3, attacks at s0,s3,s6,s9,s12,s14.
  VERSION B (Passacaglia): ANCHOR voice fixed pitch every even sub (d=2) + MOVING voice
    traces scale step-by-step every odd sub (d=2). Closed loop = starts/ends same pitch.
    Inversion rule: next section flips direction. Zero new material, completely different feel.

T5 CHORD CASCADE (climax texture, use ONCE):
  4 triads/bar. Each = 3 notes (root+3rd+5th), d=3, attacks at s2,s5,s8,s11.
  MINOR chords: triads ASCEND register (seeking). MAJOR chords: DESCEND (releasing).
  Must be preceded by T4 (interval weave) for at least 4-8 bars. Never jump straight to T5.

T6 CONVERGENCE: RH drops to LH register, plays same texture. Melody disappears.
  Duration: 12-30 bars. When melody returns after convergence, it hits harder than first time.

T7 POLYPHONIC (Una Mattina style): multiple independent voices at different registers.
  Each voice has its own rhythm, duration, and arc. No single "melody" + "accompaniment."
  Capsule structure: fixed bar-count units (2-bar, 4-bar) repeat with internal variation.
  Grammar changes between sections — what a note means in section 1 ≠ section 2.

THREE UNIVERSAL ARC LAWS (apply to every track regardless of style):
  1. The piece must have an emotional beginning, middle, and end.
     The listener must feel the track went somewhere and came home.
  2. The richest, most complex, most registrally extreme moment

  3. After the peak, the piece must return to something simpler
     before ending. Abrupt endings after climax feel unresolved.

EVOLUTION TOOLS (one per section change — never two simultaneously):
  1. Register shift — same content, one octave higher (or lower for return).
  2. Texture densification — Silence→T1→T2→T3→T4→T5 (reverse for return).
  3. Voice thickening — single note → interval pair → triad.
  4. Direction inversion — descending becomes ascending. Same notes, zero new material.
  5. Arpeggio expansion — 3-note gains SUMMIT note at s0 and s12.
  6. Cross-bar hold — d>SPB note freezes melody while LH continues. "Fog" texture.
  7. Anchor swap — LH→Type 4 drone. RH immediately starts continuous d=1/d=2 rhythm.
  8. Convergence — RH drops to LH register. Melody disappears.
  9. Motif compression — reduce tail from d=12 to d=4. Pre-climax urgency.
  10. Capsule length change — double or halve the unit length. Changes perceived time.
  11. Isolated cadence — inside continuous arpeggio, ONE bar of 2-4 long held notes.
